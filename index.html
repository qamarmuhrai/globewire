Explosion and Fire at Alberta Military Base Kill at Least Seven, Officials Say

An explosion and fire at Canadian Forces Base Wainwright in Alberta on Saturday killed at least seven people and injured dozens more, according to Global News citing military sources — in what the town’s mayor called the largest mass-casualty event the community has ever seen. Defence Minister David McGuinty confirmed there were fatalities and injuries but gave no official toll, describing the situation as “very, very active and delicate” and saying he could not recall an incident like it in recent memory. Military sources said a live-fire training exercise struck a gas line or gas well, triggering the blast, though officials have not publicly confirmed that account. High winds drove the fire beyond the base perimeter, and hospitals in Edmonton, about 180 km away, activated mass-casualty protocols as facilities near the base were overwhelmed. Prime Minister Mark Carney said his thoughts were with “all the brave women and men affected,” while Alberta Premier Danielle Smith called the fire “significant and tragic.”
