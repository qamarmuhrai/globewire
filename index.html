Latest · Europe · Spain Train Crash28 October 2026

Spain Train Crash Toll Rises to at Least 68 — Minister Blames Erroneous Restart Past a Red Signal After a Computer Failure

The death toll from Wednesday morning's collision between two high-speed trains near Urda in Spain's Toledo province has risen to at least 68, with dozens injured, officials said — up from 46 feared dead earlier in the day. Transport Minister Óscar Puente told a news conference in Madrid that a computer communications failure had automatically stopped traffic and issued a red signal because a train was stationary ahead, but that an erroneous decision was then made to restart a Renfe service, which ran into the back of the stationary Iryo train on the Madrid–Seville line at about 9:15 a.m. Two carriages derailed and fell down an embankment; together the trains carried 544 passengers. Rescue crews continued searching the two most badly damaged carriages, the Spanish Football Federation postponed all 24 Copa del Rey first-round matches scheduled for Wednesday as a mark of respect, rail services between Madrid and Andalusia remained disrupted, and a rail drivers' strike was planned for Thursday. It is Spain's second deadly crash on the line in less than a year.

Latest · Middle East · Hormuz Blockade28 October 2026

US Enforces Blockade of All Iranian Ports as Regional Threat Level Hits "Severe" — Neutral Ships Not Exempt From Checks

The United States is enforcing a blockade of all Iranian ports and coastlines, applying to every vessel regardless of flag or ownership, according to a maritime security advisory issued on Wednesday by the Joint Maritime Information Center. The notice, JMIC 016-26, issued with US Naval Forces Central Command guidance, covers the Gulf, the Strait of Hormuz, the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea and puts the regional threat level at "severe," saying the measure responds to what Washington calls Iran's continued aggression and attempts to control the strait in violation of international law. Neutral vessels — including ships carrying neutral cargo or sailing in ballast between neutral ports — may still be questioned, visited and searched, and are told to keep a continuous listening watch on VHF Channel 16, follow all directions from US forces and be ready to alter course or stop. Ships that do not need to transit the area are urged to stay well clear. Separately, Iran's army said a tanker that had switched off its tracking systems and tried to cross a prohibited area east of Hormuz struck naval mines and caught fire.

Latest · Middle East · Gulf Diplomacy28 October 2026

Gulf States Push a Multi-Part Peace Plan to Avert a Renewed US–Iran War — Hormuz Reopening Linked to Sanctions Relief

Gulf countries have intensified efforts to prevent a renewed war between the United States and Iran, working on a multilateral plan that would link the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz to sanctions relief, limits on Houthi military activity in Yemen and a wider regional security framework, according to reporting cited by UrduPoint from The Guardian on 28 October. Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar and Oman are among those involved, with Pakistan and Türkiye also taking part in talks; Iran has signalled the waterway could reopen within four days of sanctions being lifted. Tehran, however, disputed reports of a new Qatari peace draft and warned of energy-market consequences if strikes resume, while a claimed Houthi attack on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery pushed Brent crude above $115 a barrel in Wednesday trading. UAE diplomatic adviser Anwar Gargash warned that wars create more crises than they solve and said diplomacy was the way out. Washington is said to be aware of the initiative but seeking additional concessions, including inspections of Iran's nuclear facilities.

Latest · Middle East · Israel Election28 October 2026

With 85.6% Counted, Netanyahu's Bloc Has 53 Seats, Eisenkot's Bloc 48 and Arab Parties a Historic 19 — Herzog Urges Parties to Avoid Another Election

Israel's Central Elections Committee said that with 85.6% of votes counted — 93% of ordinary ballots — Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc has 53 seats, the bloc led by Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar party has 48, and Arab parties have a historic high of 19, meaning parties opposed to Netanyahu together hold 67 of the Knesset's 120 seats, though neither main bloc can govern alone without Arab support that both have ruled out. Likud remains the largest party on 26 seats, followed by Yashar on 21, with Together and the Joint List on 12 each and the United Arab List on seven. About 600,000 double-envelope votes — including soldiers' ballots — are still to be counted over the coming days and could shift the arithmetic. President Isaac Herzog said he would begin consultations next week and urged leaders to "come with an open mind" and do everything possible to avoid another election. Arab leaders said their turnout had blocked a Netanyahu majority and called for a Jewish–Arab partnership, while President Donald Trump claimed his endorsement had turned the tide for Netanyahu.

Latest · Americas · Brazil28 October 2026

Flávio Bolsonaro Lands in Miami After Fleeing Brazil Despite a Court Order to Surrender His Passports

Defeated Brazilian presidential candidate Flávio Bolsonaro landed in Miami on Wednesday morning with his wife and two daughters, less than three days after losing Sunday's runoff to incumbent Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, who won with 51.79% of the vote. Bolsonaro, the son of jailed former president Jair Bolsonaro, had refused to concede and said he would seek annulment of the result, then was ordered by Supreme Court Justice Alexandre de Moraes to surrender his passports in a money-laundering investigation — an order a police source said led to both his Brazilian and diplomatic passports being cancelled, with no formal record of his departure. Argentine migration officials said the family crossed by the Tancredo Neves bridge late Tuesday and boarded a private flight to Ecuador, continuing to Florida; he also holds Italian nationality, granted in 2023. The US State Department said it would issue visas to the family, and President Trump said he would approve. Lula called fleeing the country "shameful," and Bolsonaro's own campaign manager said he had no news of his whereabouts beyond a brief text message.

Latest · Europe · UK Budget28 October 2026

UK Finance Minister Healey Delivers a Cautious First Budget for the Burnham Government — £15 Billion Headroom and £10 Billion More for Ukraine

British finance minister John Healey unveiled his first budget on Wednesday, a cautious package of support for households and businesses designed to avoid unsettling bond markets, as Prime Minister Andy Burnham's government juggles defence commitments and a cost-of-living squeeze driven largely by the US–Iran war. Healey said fiscal headroom stood at £15 billion, in line with a Reuters poll but down from £23.6 billion in March; bond yields eased slightly, sterling slipped 0.1% to $1.3298, bank shares rose 3.3–3.6% after a smaller-than-feared tax rise on profits, and one strategist called it "reassuringly dull." Healey also announced £10 billion in additional economic and military support for Ukraine — loans, export finance guarantees and £500 million in urgent winter military funding to plug air-defence gaps — saying President Volodymyr Zelenskiy had told him and Burnham, "you've made my day." Details of how Britain will reach NATO's 3.5%-of-GDP defence target by 2035 are expected to wait until next year.

Latest · Asia-Pacific · Cyclone Arnab28 October 2026

Cyclone Arnab Kills Five in Andhra Pradesh, Damages Crops Across 80,500 Hectares — More Than 180,000 People Evacuated

Five people were killed in rain-related incidents as very severe cyclonic storm Arnab battered northern Andhra Pradesh overnight with winds gusting to 150 km/h, uprooting more than 1,000 trees, officials reported on Wednesday — an official toll that has risen through the day, with the National Disaster Response Force earlier confirming three deaths, including a man killed by a falling tree in Bhimavaram and two people electrocuted. The storm crossed the coast near Narsapur in the early hours and weakened inland, but more than 42 weather stations in Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram and Anakapalli recorded over 200 mm of rain in 24 hours, with Garividi highest at 260 mm. Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu told the federal home minister that paddy across about 70,000 hectares and horticulture across 10,500 hectares were inundated, at least 180 houses were damaged, and announced relief including rice and essentials for affected families and ₹5 lakh for the families of those killed. More than 180,000 people were evacuated in Andhra Pradesh and Odisha, 38 flights were cancelled at Visakhapatnam, and dozens of trains were cancelled or rescheduled.

Ukraine Downs 71 of 88 Russian Drones Overnight Alongside a Ballistic Missile — 191 Clashes Across the Front, Heaviest at Pokrovsk

Russian forces attacked Ukraine overnight with an Iskander-M/S-400 ballistic missile and 88 drones — including 38 jet-powered Shahed types, Gerbera and decoy aircraft — and Ukrainian air defences destroyed or jammed 71 of them by 8 a.m., the Air Force said, with hits recorded at eight locations and debris falling at two. Ukrainian missile forces and artillery struck a Russian drone command post. Kyiv's daily estimate put Russian losses at 1,510 personnel and 266 vehicles in the past 24 hours, figures that cannot be independently verified. Several Russian attack drones were still airborne as the Air Force issued its morning update and combat operations were continuing.

Latest · Europe · Serbia28 October 2026

Policeman Shoots Dead a Knife Attacker Outside the Israeli Embassy in Belgrade — Serbia Raises Threat Level to Red

An attacker armed with two knives wounded a policeman guarding the Israeli embassy in Belgrade overnight before the officer shot him dead, Serbia's outgoing interior minister Ivica Dačić said on Wednesday. Dačić said the assailant, a minor of so far undetermined identity believed to be from the western city of Šabac, rushed at a Gendarmerie officer shouting "Allahu Akbar." Prosecutors said the black-clad attacker opened the guard-house door and threw one knife, hitting the officer in the left cheek, before the officer fired several shots; his action was qualified as justified self-defence and the investigation is continuing. Israel's foreign ministry called it an attempted terrorist attack and said no embassy personnel were hurt. Dačić raised Serbia's security threat level to red, the highest, with increased police presence at key locations. It is the second attack at the embassy in two years, after a crossbow attack wounded an officer in June 2024, and it comes as Serbia's outgoing administration serves in a caretaker role after Sunday's election.

Latest · Americas · US Justice Department28 October 2026

Grand Jury Refuses to Indict Cassidy Hutchinson Over Her January 6 Testimony — the Latest Setback for Prosecutors a Week Before the Midterms

A federal grand jury in Washington has declined to indict former White House aide Cassidy Hutchinson, the January 6 committee's star witness, whom the Justice Department investigated over allegations she lied under oath in her 2022 testimony, according to reports by the Associated Press and NBC News cited on Wednesday. The decision — a "no bill" — does not establish whether her testimony was truthful, and prosecutors could still present the case to another grand jury; Hutchinson declined to comment through her lawyer and the department had not responded to requests for comment by Wednesday morning. The refusal adds to a run of grand-jury setbacks in cases against figures President Donald Trump has cast as opponents, including repeated refusals to re-indict New York Attorney General Letitia James and a February refusal to indict six Democratic lawmakers over a video urging troops to refuse unlawful orders, while former FBI director James Comey faces a third set of charges filed this week. The timing, six days before the 3 November midterms, has made the department's pursuit of Trump's critics itself a campaign issue.
