Latest · Americas · United States — Air Force Cloud Breach27 November 2026

Hackers Breach US Air Force Flagship Cloud One Platform, Sources Say

Intruders have broken into Cloud One, the US Air Force’s flagship cloud computing platform, according to three people familiar with the matter, raising concern over a system that handles large volumes of sensitive tactical, personnel and logistics information. The intrusion was uncovered within the past month or so while the platform was being upgraded, two of the people said, and the discovery has caused disruption across the service. The identity of the hackers, the full extent of the access and whether any data was taken have not yet been established. The case adds to a run of security incidents involving defence technology systems, where a single cloud environment can connect many units and programmes at once.

Latest · Middle East · Iraq — State Authority Drive27 November 2026

Trump Backs Iraqi Prime Minister’s Push to Bring All Weapons Under State Control

President Donald Trump on Friday publicly backed Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi’s effort to assert state authority across the country, including stopping the smuggling of Iraqi oil, ending corruption and bringing all weapons under the exclusive control of the state. The endorsement, posted on Truth Social, puts Washington behind a drive that brings Baghdad into direct confrontation with Iran-aligned armed groups and with Kurdish authorities. On Thursday Iraq closed two border crossings in the Kurdistan region, one with Syria and one with Iran, after Zaidi ordered federal authorities to take over crossings run by the semi-autonomous regional government. Iraq’s highest court ruled on Wednesday that Baghdad can deploy federal forces along all international borders, including in the Kurdistan region.

Latest · Middle East · Defence — Mecca Pact Talks Open in Islamabad27 November 2026

Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Türkiye Open Islamabad Talks on Putting Mecca Defence Pact Into Practice

Senior officials from Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Türkiye began a two-day meeting in Islamabad on Friday to turn their Mecca Joint Defence Agreement into working arrangements, officials said. It is the third session of the pact’s Strategic Political and Defence Committee, which brings together the three countries’ foreign and defence ministers and senior military officials. The agenda is expected to cover standard operating procedures, an implementation mechanism and coordination between political, defence and military institutions, as well as the conflict between Saudi Arabia and Yemen’s Iran-backed Houthis. Defence Minister Khawaja Asif said any threat to Saudi Arabia is a threat to Pakistan and Türkiye under the pact. The agreement, signed in Mecca on 7 August, treats an armed attack on one member as an attack on all three. Türkiye completed its ratification on Friday when President Recep Tayyip Erdogan formally signed off on parliament’s approval. Islamabad declared a local holiday and imposed traffic restrictions around the Red Zone for the gathering.

Latest · Global Affairs · Refugees — Shelter Closures27 November 2026

Funding Cuts Have Closed Nearly Half of Shelters for Displaced Women, UN Agency Warns

Nearly half of the safe spaces for displaced women and girls surveyed across more than a dozen countries have shut since the start of last year because of funding cuts, the UN refugee agency said on Friday, warning that survivors now have fewer places to report abuse and seek help. Of 374 such spaces, 171 closed last year and more followed in 2026 across 14 operations, including conflict-affected Sudan, Yemen and the Democratic Republic of Congo, while 203 remained open. The agency said its programmes against gender-based violence received less than a third of the funding requested last year and less than a quarter so far in 2026, leaving 5.6 million forcibly displaced and stateless people without prevention and response services in 2025. Officials said most operations also report delays in case management and rising caseloads per worker as staffing falls.

Latest · Americas · United States — Thanksgiving Storm Aftermath26 November 2026

Thanksgiving Storm Leaves Over 900,000 Without Power and Snarls East Coast Travel

A powerful coastal storm disrupted Thanksgiving across much of the US East Coast on Thursday, bringing wind gusts of up to 70 mph, heavy rain and coastal flooding linked to king tides. At the peak more than 900,000 utility customers were without power, with the largest outages in Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Maryland and Virginia, and more than 100,000 further outages in Missouri after snow fell there. FlightAware counted more than 400 US flight cancellations and well over 1,600 delays by Thursday afternoon, with Washington and New York area airports among the hardest hit. New York City postponed the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade because of the weather, rescheduling it to Saturday, and governors in New York and New Jersey declared states of emergency. Forecasters expected the heaviest rain and strongest winds to ease overnight as the system moved into New England.

Latest · Europe · Spain — Election Finale27 November 2026

Sánchez Makes Final Push as Spain Heads Into Election Expected to Favour the Right

Spain’s Socialist Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez spent Friday, the last day of campaigning, trying to mobilise left-wing voters ahead of Sunday’s snap election, which polls suggest could bring a conservative and far-right coalition to power. Sánchez called the vote, originally due in 2027, after parliament rejected his housing decrees on 2 October. Most polls put the conservative People’s Party first at around 31 percent, with the far-right Vox at roughly 18 percent and the Socialists close behind at almost 30 percent — making a PP-Vox combination the only one projected to reach an absolute majority in the 350-seat lower house. Such a result would give a far-right party a cabinet role for the first time since Spain’s transition to democracy. Sánchez held his closing rally in Madrid, PP leader Alberto Núñez Feijóo closed in Seville, and Vox leader Santiago Abascal closed in Valencia.

Zelenskyy Warns Russia Is Preparing Another Mass Strike as Sides Exchange Fallen Soldiers

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy warned in his evening address on Thursday that Ukraine has information Russia is preparing another massive strike in the coming days, urging people to pay close attention to missile alerts. He said repair crews were working to restore electricity and heat after earlier attacks in regions including Dnipropetrovsk, Kharkiv, Odesa, Sumy, Donetsk and Mykolaiv, and that Kyiv is seeking further weapons from partners to protect infrastructure, particularly against drones. Latest · Africa · Egypt — Cairo Tanker Fire26 November 2026

Egypt Detains Driver After Cairo Ring Road Tanker Fire Kills 31 and Destroys 27 Vehicles

Egyptian prosecutors have ordered the detention of a 21-year-old driver suspected of causing the collision that overturned a fuel tanker on Cairo’s ring road on Wednesday evening, sparking a rush-hour fire that killed 31 people. The health ministry put the final toll at 31 dead and 26 injured. According to prosecutors, the driver was speeding and weaving between lanes when he struck the tanker’s rear, bursting a tyre and sending it into a concrete barrier, where it overturned, spilled fuel and caught fire in the al-Waraq district of Giza. The blaze spread to passing traffic and destroyed 27 vehicles. The driver was sent for forensic testing, including for drugs. Lawmakers demanded accountability on Thursday, and the disaster — described as Egypt’s deadliest fuel tanker explosion on record — has renewed anger over road safety in a country that recorded 5,829 road deaths in 2025.

Latest · Asia-Pacific · Pakistan — Karachi Building Collapse27 November 2026

Karachi Building Collapse Toll Rises to Seven as Rescuers Recover Three More Bodies

The death toll from the collapse of an under-construction six-storey building near Karachi’s Baloch Colony rose to seven on Friday after rescue workers recovered three more bodies from the rubble, officials said. The building, reportedly being built illegally on a 600-square-yard residential plot in the Karachi Administration Employees Housing Society where only a ground-plus-one-storey structure had been approved, collapsed on Wednesday with more than 20 workers inside. Three people died and 14 were injured on the first day, and a fourth body was recovered on Thursday. Rescue 1122 said more bodies had been located and the recovery operation, under way for about 45 hours, was continuing. Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah has removed senior officials, suspended 16 building-control officers, ordered the arrest of the owner and builder, and halted construction on 123 buildings being built under stay orders; 14 buildings in the society have been sealed for demolition.

Latest · Global Affairs · Technology — Orbital Data Centres26 November 2026

US Regulators Authorise SpaceX to Deploy One Million Satellites for Data Centres in Space

The US Federal Communications Commission has authorised SpaceX to deploy up to one million satellites for a constellation called Starmind, intended to work as orbital data centres processing large amounts of data for use on Earth. The FCC described the approval, announced on Wednesday, as a historic first and said the solar-powered modules would help meet rapidly growing demand for artificial intelligence computing. Under the licence, SpaceX must place at least one authorised satellite in orbit by November 2033, 100,000 by November 2035, half the constellation by November 2038 and the full fleet by November 2040; the company has said it hopes to begin launching in 2027. For scale, only about 17,000 satellites are currently in orbit. SpaceX has cautioned in filings that the technology is unproven and may not prove commercially viable, and scientists have raised concerns about views of the night sky, light pollution and orbital debris.
