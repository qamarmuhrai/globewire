Latest · Middle East · Ras Tanura & Riyadh — Four Drones Intercepted25 November 2026

Houthis Say They Attacked a Saudi Oil Refinery and Riyadh’s Financial District

Yemen’s Houthi movement said on Wednesday that it had attacked Saudi Arabia’s Ras Tanura oil refinery and the financial district of the capital, Riyadh, in a significant widening of its campaign against the kingdom. The Saudi-led coalition said earlier on Wednesday that air defences had intercepted and destroyed four drones over Ras Tanura, in the Eastern Province. Saudi civil defence said 11 people were wounded on Tuesday when a strike hit a tower in Riyadh’s financial district. The claims deepen an escalation that has already seen the Houthis threaten Saudi Red Sea ports and the coalition answer with strikes inside Yemen.

Source: Reuters
Latest · Americas · Cartagena — One Captor Killed in Rescue25 November 2026

All Hostages Freed as Colombian Forces Storm Cartagena Supermarket

Colombian security forces stormed a supermarket in the Caribbean city of Cartagena on Wednesday, ending a day-long hostage siege and rescuing everyone held inside, AFP reported. President Abelardo De La Espriella said all of the hostages were brought out safely and that one of the captors was killed during the operation. The rescue closes a tense standoff that had gripped the port city since the gunmen seized the store a day earlier.

Source: Reuters / AFP
Latest · Africa · Mali & the Sahel — 235 Drone Attacks Counted Since 202325 November 2026

Cheap Drones Carry Ukraine-Style Warfare to Insurgents Across Africa

Armed groups across West Africa’s Sahel are using off-the-shelf drones costing only a few hundred dollars to challenge armies that once ruled the skies with jets and helicopters, a Reuters investigation published on Wednesday found. In Mali, separatists and an al Qaeda affiliate filmed first-person-view drone strikes on the army and its Russian paramilitary backers during a joint assault in April; previously unpublished ACLED data counts 235 drone attacks across four West African countries since 2023. Reuters reporting indicates Ukrainian trainers helped prepare Mali’s separatists, part of a wider pattern of Kyiv striking at Russian forces far from home — including the sinking of a Russian warship in a Libyan port on Sunday. Kyiv has denied supporting illegal armed groups, and Moscow did not comment.

Source: Reuters

US Shelves Plan to Tempt Russia With Business Deals to End Ukraine War, Report Says

The Trump administration has quietly set aside proposals to use business deals as an inducement for Russia to end its war in Ukraine, the Financial Times reported on Wednesday. The ideas, which included energy and aviation ventures, were shelved after the Kremlin showed little interest in taking them up. Reuters said it could not immediately verify the report. The apparent shift removes one of the more unconventional levers Washington had considered as the war grinds through its fourth year.

Latest · Asia-Pacific · Beijing — Fourth Politburo Member Probed Since 202525 November 2026

China’s Politburo Shrinks as Graft Crackdown Reaches Shanghai’s Party Chief

China’s anti-corruption watchdog has opened an investigation into Chen Jining, Shanghai’s Communist Party chief and a member of the ruling Politburo, making him the fourth member of the elite 24-strong body to face a graft probe since 2025, Reuters reported on Wednesday. The inquiry follows the earlier downfalls of He Weidong in 2025 and of Zhang Youxia and Ma Xingrui this year, steadily thinning the leadership tier formed in 2022. Chen had been widely tipped as a candidate for promotion to the Politburo Standing Committee in next year’s reshuffle, and Shanghai’s senior officials have publicly pledged loyalty to Beijing since the probe was announced.

Latest · Asia-Pacific · Hong Kong — 168 Killed in City’s Deadliest Fire Since 194825 November 2026

One Year After the Wang Fuk Court Fire, Hong Kong Survivors Still Ask Who Was Responsible

A year after an inferno tore through Hong Kong’s Wang Fuk Court housing estate, killing 168 people in the city’s deadliest fire since 1948, survivors and bereaved families are still pressing for accountability. The blaze ripped through seven of the estate’s eight 31-storey blocks in Tai Po while renovation work was under way, displacing thousands. Many former residents are expected to return to the blackened towers on Thursday to mark the anniversary; the government says it will hold no official memorial but will not stop citizens gathering. An independent report released last month found four government departments partly responsible, and the housing secretary has resigned — steps many families say do not go far enough.

Restaurant Explosion Kills at Least Six in a Crowded Hanoi Neighbourhood

An explosion at a restaurant in Hanoi’s Dong Da district killed at least six people and injured 10 others on Tuesday, Vietnamese authorities said. The blast, which struck at about 8 a.m. as the eatery filled with customers, shattered windows, tore metal shutters from their frames and heavily damaged surrounding homes. Vietnam’s Ministry of Public Security said early findings pointed to cooking gas as the cause, while rescue teams continued searching the wreckage for anyone trapped inside.

Latest · Asia-Pacific · Jiuquan — First Foreign Astronaut Aboard Tiangong25 November 2026

China’s Shenzhou-24 Lifts Off With a Pakistani Astronaut, the First Foreigner Bound for Tiangong

China’s Shenzhou-24 spacecraft blasted off on Wednesday morning carrying a three-person crew that includes Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, a 40-year-old Pakistani pilot who becomes the first foreign astronaut to board the Tiangong space station. The craft rode a Long March-2F rocket from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Centre in northwest China. Ali, flying as a payload specialist, is expected to spend five to ten days at the station conducting experiments. Beijing is promoting Tiangong as a hub for international cooperation in orbit, a role that could grow if the International Space Station is retired around 2030 as planned.

Russia Extradites Chinese National Wanted Over $2.3 Billion in Illegal Deposits

Russia has extradited to China a Chinese national wanted for illegally taking public deposits worth more than 15 billion yuan, about $2.25 billion, China’s official Xinhua news agency said on Tuesday. China and US Hold Farm Trade Talks in Beijing Under New Board of Trade

China and the United States have held the first meeting of an agriculture working group set up under the two countries’ Board of Trade, China’s Commerce Ministry said. Officials met in Beijing on Monday and Tuesday to discuss farm trade and market access, keeping a channel of economic dialogue open even as Washington weighs fresh tariff measures following a trade investigation into alleged excess industrial capacity among major trading partners. Agricultural goods have long been among the most politically sensitive items in the two economies’ trade relationship.
