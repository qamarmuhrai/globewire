Middle East12 October 2026

Four Regional Powers Condemn Attacks on Riyadh Airport

Turkey, Pakistan, Egypt and Saudi Arabia issued a joint condemnation after a second deadly Houthi attack on Riyadh’s international airport. Saudi authorities said Sunday’s strike killed one person and injured nearly a dozen, following a larger attack the previous day; the casualty figures have not been independently verified.

Source: Reuters
Middle East12 October 2026

Iran Says US Reply on Hormuz Proposal Falls Short

Iran’s security chief said Washington’s response to Tehran’s seven-condition plan for reopening the Strait of Hormuz was insufficient. Messages have passed through Qatari intermediaries, but Iran says talks and normal access through the waterway will not resume until its conditions are met.

Source: Reuters via BusinessLine
Europe12 October 2026

French Poll Puts Le Pen and Mélenchon in Run-Off

A Toluna Harris Interactive survey conducted from 5 to 8 October places Marine Le Pen first in every tested first-round scenario and Jean-Luc Mélenchon second. The poll projects Le Pen winning a head-to-head contest, while highlighting the pressure on France’s weakened political centre.

Source: Reuters
Africa12 October 2026

Ethiopia Opens Its First Gold Refinery

Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed said a $50 million joint venture has begun operating as Ethiopia’s first gold refinery. The state-majority project is intended to process domestic and regional output, adding services from testing and refining to bar minting.

Source: Reuters
Americas12 October 2026

Mexico Braces as Category 4 Hurricane Simon Nears

President Claudia Sheinbaum urged residents in four Pacific-coast states to remain sheltered as Hurricane Simon approached west-central Mexico. The storm carried maximum sustained winds of 145 mph in the cited advisory, with flooding, storm surge and destructive waves expected even if its centre stayed offshore.

Source: Reuters via GMA News
Asia-Pacific12 October 2026

North Korea Launches Multiple Ballistic Missiles

South Korea’s military said several missiles were fired from the Pyongyang area toward waters east of the peninsula on Monday morning. Some travelled more than 900 kilometres, according to Seoul, which convened an emergency security meeting while analysts examined the weapons’ specifications.

Source: Channel News Asia
Global Economy11 October 2026

Energy Shock and Debt Dominate Bangkok Finance Meetings

Finance officials gathering for the IMF and World Bank annual meetings face rising energy costs, heavy public debt and weak global growth. The institutions are monitoring how the Iran conflict, strained fuel supplies and higher borrowing costs are combining to pressure lower-income economies.

Source: Reuters via CNBC Africa
South Asia12 October 2026

Pakistan Targets Growth Above 4% and New Privatisations

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb told Reuters that Pakistan still expects economic growth above 4% this fiscal year despite costly energy imports. He said the government aims to privatise five or six entities and raise more than $1 billion, while talks continue over a proposed US exchange-stabilisation facility.

Source: Reuters
