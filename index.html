Bangladesh Battles World’s Worst Measles Outbreak as Deaths Pass 1,100

Bangladesh has recorded about 202,000 suspected measles cases since mid-March, with 22,000 confirmed in laboratories and 1,147 deaths, the World Health Organization said. Children under five account for 94% of the deaths. Health experts say routine vaccination slipped in 2025 below the 95% coverage needed to stop outbreaks, while incomplete records left millions of children hard to reach — a warning, they say, for any country where immunisation falters.
