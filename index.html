Latest · Middle East · Kuwait — Drone Attack Fallout27 November 2026

Kuwait Summons Iran’s Ambassador as Tehran Denies Role in Drone Attack That Killed Two Italians

Kuwait’s foreign ministry said on Friday it had summoned Iran’s ambassador, Mohammad Totonji, and handed him a formal protest over continued Iranian attacks, a day after a drone strike on a vital site killed two Italian nationals in the first fatal incident of its kind in the Gulf state for months. Kuwaiti authorities say the men died from falling debris while incoming drones were being intercepted. German defence contractor Rheinmetall confirmed the victims were its employees, who were maintaining air-defence equipment; Italian media named them as Antonio Saccoccio, 65, and Emanuele Lo Russo, 36. Iran’s Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi called the incident “suspicious” and a “pre-cooked recipe for war,” denying any Iranian role and suggesting a false-flag operation, while Tehran has offered a joint investigation. In a related development, Qatar condemned Thursday’s projectile attack on Saudi Aramco’s refinery in Jazan, which killed a Nepali worker, and oil prices rose towards $97 a barrel amid tanker incidents in the Strait of Hormuz.

Latest · Europe · European Union — Summit Ends Without Deal27 November 2026

EU Summit Ends Without Budget Deal as €45 Billion Ukraine Loan Is Put Off to December

European Union leaders ended an extraordinary summit in Brussels on Thursday night, a day earlier than planned, without agreeing either the bloc’s 2028–2034 budget or a proposed €45 billion loan to help Ukraine meet its 2027 economic and military needs. European Council President António Costa said 24 of the 27 member states backed the loan — split between €25 billion for defence and €20 billion in budget support — but Hungary, Slovakia and Czechia held out, and the loan has become entangled in the wider budget fight, with Germany arguing that access to EU funds should be tied to backing Kyiv. Costa’s compromise would cap the seven-year budget at about €1.78 trillion, below the European Commission’s original proposal of nearly €2 trillion. Ukraine faces an estimated €70 billion financing gap in 2027 even after a €90 billion EU loan already agreed for 2026–27. Leaders will return on 17–18 December, and Costa has warned the budget must be settled by the end of the year.

At Least Eight Found Dead on Migrant Boat Off Canary Islands as Daily Arrivals Hit a Record

At least eight migrants were found dead when a boat carrying about 100 people was rescued near Spain’s Canary Islands, the maritime rescue service said on Friday. The deaths came on a day when migrant arrivals in the archipelago broke the single-day record, adding to pressure on local authorities who say the surge is overwhelming reception capacity in the region. Latest · Global Affairs · Food Security — Hunger Hotspots27 November 2026

Hunger to Worsen in 16 Hotspots With Famine Risk in Sudan and South Sudan, UN Agencies Warn

Acute hunger is projected to worsen significantly in 16 hotspots between December 2026 and May 2027, the UN Food and Agriculture Organization and the World Food Programme warned on Friday, with a risk of famine identified in parts of Sudan and South Sudan. The twice-yearly Hunger Hotspots list has grown from 13 in June: Sudan, South Sudan, Somalia, parts of Nigeria and Yemen are rated of highest concern, while Afghanistan, the Democratic Republic of Congo, Haiti and Palestine are of very high concern. Conflict and armed violence drive hunger in 12 of the 16 hotspots, and El Niño has pushed Southern Africa, the Central American Dry Corridor and northeastern Uganda onto the list, with Chad added because of spillover from Sudan’s war. In Sudan, famine is a risk in 13 areas of North Darfur and South Kordofan through January 2027, and malnutrition in two displacement camps in South Darfur has already exceeded famine thresholds. The agencies warned that shrinking humanitarian funding is undermining efforts to head off catastrophe, and their joint anticipatory action plan aims to protect 9 million people in 22 countries.

Latest · Asia-Pacific · China — Markets Rally27 November 2026

Chinese Stocks Post Biggest Gain in Two Years After Beijing Vows Stronger Market Support

China’s share benchmarks jumped about 4 percent on Friday, their biggest daily rise since October 2024, after Premier Li Qiang chaired a State Council meeting that called for state-backed “national team” investors to play a bigger role in stabilising the market. The Shanghai Composite ended 4.2 percent higher and the blue-chip CSI300 rose 3.7 percent, while the tech-focused STAR 50 surged 15 percent and chip indices climbed more than 17 percent; Hong Kong’s Hang Seng gained 1.9 percent. The cabinet said capital markets are crucial to the wider economy, that Central Huijin would strengthen its role as a quasi-stabilisation fund to ensure ample liquidity, and that policies should draw more long-term funds into stocks, encourage dividends and buybacks, and guard against risks spilling in from overseas. The pledge, Beijing’s strongest yet after shares fell to 16-month lows amid weak demand, a long property downturn and trade-war uncertainty, also lifted the yuan to its strongest in three weeks, though some analysts cautioned that state buying alone may not sustain a rally.

Mexico Files Formal Complaint With US Over DEA Tip Line on Alleged Traffickers and Officials

Mexico has filed a formal complaint with the United States over a tip line run by the US Drug Enforcement Administration that seeks information on suspected drug traffickers and allegedly corrupt Mexican officials, President Claudia Sheinbaum said on Friday. The move puts a fresh strain on cross-border security cooperation, a relationship already sensitive in Mexico City whenever US agencies are seen to be operating on matters touching Mexican sovereignty and public officials. Sheinbaum announced the complaint publicly, signalling that her government wants the channel addressed through diplomatic channels rather than left as a unilateral US initiative.

Latest · Asia-Pacific · India — Cyber Fraud Rescue27 November 2026

Indian Police Rescue 164 Indonesians and Malaysians Held by Cyber Fraud Operation Near Delhi

Police in India say they have rescued dozens of Indonesian and Malaysian nationals who were illegally confined and forced to take part in a cyber fraud operation in a suburb of the capital, Delhi. In a statement issued late on Thursday, Delhi police put the total rescued at 164 people — 92 Indonesians and 72 Malaysians. The case adds to a growing regional pattern in which workers are lured abroad with job offers and then coerced into running online scams, a trade that governments across South and Southeast Asia have been trying to dismantle through cross-border police work.

Latest · Africa · Kenya — Cult Leader Convicted27 November 2026

Kenyan Starvation Cult Leader Found Guilty of Manslaughter Over 123 Followers’ Deaths

A Kenyan court on Friday found starvation cult leader Paul Mackenzie guilty of manslaughter for the deaths of 123 of his followers, the latest conviction arising from one of the deadliest cult-related massacres in modern times. The case centred on followers who died after being drawn into Mackenzie’s movement, which authorities say persuaded adherents to starve themselves. The verdict adds a further judgment against Mackenzie in a prosecution that has unfolded in stages as investigators worked through the scale of the deaths.
