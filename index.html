Breaking · South Asia12 October 2026

Pakistan Imposes Emergency Rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Pakistan has placed Khyber Pakhtunkhwa under emergency and governor’s rule after authorities detained former chief minister Sohail Afridi, assembly speaker Babar Saleem Swati, PTI leader Junaid Akbar and other senior figures on Sunday. The interior ministry confirmed the preventive detentions to Reuters, while opposition leaders described the action as politically motivated.

Source: Dawn

Ukraine Awaits Details of Proposed Energy Ceasefire

President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said Ukraine supports a halt to strikes on energy infrastructure but is waiting for Washington to explain the terms of the arrangement announced by President Donald Trump. Zelenskiy said Russian attacks on Ukrainian energy sites were continuing despite the announcement, while Moscow had not publicly responded.

Source: Reuters
Middle East12 October 2026

Four Regional Powers Condemn Attacks on Riyadh Airport

Turkey, Pakistan, Egypt and Saudi Arabia issued a joint condemnation after a second deadly Houthi attack on Riyadh’s international airport. Saudi authorities said Sunday’s strike killed one person and injured nearly a dozen, following a larger attack the previous day; the casualty figures have not been independently verified.

Source: Reuters
Europe12 October 2026

Czech Intelligence Chief Warns of Riskier Russian Operations

Michal Koudelka, head of the Czech Security Information Service, said Russia could move toward more dangerous actions inside NATO countries that support Ukraine. He told a parliamentary security conference that possible scenarios ranged from sabotage to attacks on weapons facilities, while stressing that no immediate military threat had been identified.

Source: Reuters
Europe12 October 2026

Belgium Strike Disrupts Transport, Schools and Broadcasting

Belgian public-sector workers staged a second day of nationwide action against proposed budget cuts, disrupting rail services, local transport, schools and public broadcasting. The stoppage adds pressure on Prime Minister Bart De Wever after coalition partners failed to agree a federal budget and his State of the Union address was postponed.

Source: Reuters
Africa12 October 2026

Rights Group Reports 72 Miners Killed in Northern Sudan

Emergency Lawyers said an airstrike killed at least 72 artisanal gold miners in northern Sudan. The group blamed Egypt’s air force, an allegation Reuters could not independently verify; Egypt’s military did not comment. Sudan’s army-led government said the incident occurred near the border and that an investigation was under way.

Source: Reuters via Egypt Independent

Brazil’s presidential campaign entered its final stretch as Senator Flavio Bolsonaro and President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva courted voters in São Paulo ahead of the 25 October runoff. Bolsonaro sought endorsements from local officials, while Lula made public safety a central theme at a rally on Avenida Paulista.

South Korea’s military said several missiles were fired from the Pyongyang area toward waters east of the peninsula on Monday morning. Some travelled more than 900 kilometres, according to Seoul, which convened an emergency security meeting while analysts examined the weapons’ specifications.

Iran Says US Reply on Hormuz Proposal Falls Short

Iranian security chief Mohsen Rezaei said Washington’s response to Tehran’s seven conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz did not resolve key issues. Iran said messages continue to pass through intermediaries and that it will answer after reviewing the US position; the White House did not immediately comment.

Gunmen Kill at Least 20 in Ambush on Nigerian Traders

Armed attackers ambushed a military-escorted convoy travelling to a market in Zamfara state, killing at least 20 people, according to a witness and a military source. The reported dead included civilians and security personnel, with 19 others wounded; national military and police authorities had not immediately commented.

Source: Reuters
