Settlers Attack European Diplomats Visiting West Bank Village During Olive Harvest

Masked Israeli settlers attacked a convoy of European diplomats and officials near the West Bank village of Taybeh on Saturday, hurling stones, smashing a window of a Belgian consulate car and knocking a French town mayor to the ground. The delegation, which included France’s consul in Jerusalem and representatives from Belgium and Poland, was visiting the largely Christian village as settlers tried to stop Palestinians from gathering their olives. World Bank Chief Warns of Jobs Gap as Debt and Aid Cuts Squeeze Poor Countries

High debt levels, rising debt-service costs and a sharp drop in official development assistance are forcing developing countries to rethink how development is financed, World Bank President Ajay Banga told the IMF and World Bank annual meetings in Bangkok.
