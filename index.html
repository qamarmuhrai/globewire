Latest · Europe / Global Affairs · Abu Dhabi Talks — Prisoner Exchange11 November 2026

Russia and Ukraine Agree Their Largest Prisoner Exchange Yet in Abu Dhabi, but the Land Dispute Blocks Any Peace Breakthrough

Two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi, the first direct meeting between Russian and Ukrainian delegations in nine months, have ended with agreement on what officials describe as the biggest humanitarian release of prisoners of the war — but no movement on ending the nearly five-year invasion. Russian negotiator Vladimir Medinsky and Ukraine’s lead Rustem Umerov both confirmed the planned swap, and Russian officials said it could involve more than 1,000 people on each side and take place within two to three weeks once numbers are finalised. US envoy Jared Kushner called the sessions productive and said he and fellow envoy Steve Witkoff would travel to Moscow on Thursday to brief President Vladimir Putin, then on to Dublin to meet President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. On the core question of territory neither side shifted: Moscow continues to demand Ukrainian withdrawals from parts of the Donbas and two other partly occupied regions, while Kyiv calls the exchange itself the talks’ main result.

Latest · Global Affairs · COP31 — Antalya Leaders’ Summit12 November 2026

More Than 100 Leaders Open the COP31 Summit in Antalya With a New Sales Pitch: Climate Action as a Way to Cut Living Costs

Leaders from more than 100 countries have gathered in the Turkish resort city of Antalya for the leaders’ summit of COP31, the UN climate conference running from 9 to 20 November, and organisers are framing the case for action in economic terms — lower bills and stronger growth — as wars and cost-of-living crises push global warming down government agendas. Host President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan opened proceedings and hosted a leader-level session on electrification alongside UN Secretary-General António Guterres, who also pressed for universal early-warning systems covering every person on Earth by the end of 2027 and for global methane standards. The presidency unveiled the Antalya Green Industrialisation Compact to speed investment in low-emissions industry, while a zero-waste programme set targets to halve the growth of municipal waste by 2035. The summit is also serving as a diplomatic crossroads: US President Donald Trump is absent, China is represented by Vice President Han Zheng, and Pakistan’s prime minister used a clean-energy session to seek about $188 billion in concessional finance for his country’s transition by 2035.

Latest · Americas · New York — Building Collapse12 November 2026

Brooklyn Building Collapse Kills at Least Two and Injures 17 as Rescuers Dig Through Rubble Beside a Construction Site

A three-storey mixed-use building in Brooklyn’s Flatbush neighbourhood collapsed into an adjoining excavation site on Wednesday afternoon, killing at least two people and injuring 17, including seven children, in one of New York’s worst building disasters in years. The fire department was called just after 4 p.m. local time, and by evening some 250 firefighters and paramedics were working the scene, pulling debris away bucket by bucket with help from a search dog and drone footage as officials warned the operation could last several days. Four of the injured were in critical condition and one firefighter was treated for minor injuries. The four-unit building dated from 1930 and stood at the edge of a lot where another structure had been demolished for a housing project; Mayor Zohran Mamdani said the Department of Buildings and law enforcement would investigate the cause, which remains unknown. City records show a demolition contractor at the neighbouring site was cited in April for continuing work despite a stop-work order.

Latest · Europe · School Bomb Threats — Hoax Wave11 November 2026

Hoax Bomb Threats Hit More Than 6,000 Schools in Italy, With Evacuations and Parallel Alerts Across Britain and Ireland

Threatening emails claiming explosive devices had been planted were sent to thousands of schools across Europe on Wednesday, forcing evacuations and a continent-wide security response before authorities concluded the messages were a hoax. In Italy more than 6,000 schools received the threat and at least 300 were evacuated while police checked buildings. A similar message reached schools in Scotland, Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland, where police said the warning originated from outside the United Kingdom and treated it as a hoax, and alerts were also reported elsewhere on the continent. The wave follows days of bomb threats against European transport and media organisations, and officials are investigating whether the campaigns are connected. No devices were found, and classes were expected to resume as police gave schools the all-clear.

Latest · Middle East · Saudi Arabia — Missiles Intercepted12 November 2026

Saudi Arabia Says Debris From Two Intercepted Ballistic Missiles Fell in the Mecca Region as Houthi Attacks Escalate

Saudi Arabia says debris from two ballistic missiles intercepted by its air defences fell in several places in the Mecca region on Wednesday, including Sabuhah village, as its conflict with Yemen’s Iran-aligned Houthi movement intensifies again. The kingdom’s civil defence, in a statement issued early on Thursday, called the targeting of civilian objects a flagrant violation of international humanitarian law. The Houthis have repeatedly denied Saudi accusations that they aim at Mecca and Medina, Islam’s two holiest cities, saying their operations are directed at military and strategic targets. A UN-brokered truce in 2022 had largely frozen the war that began after the Houthis seized Sanaa in 2014 and Saudi Arabia intervened the following year, but missile and drone attacks on Saudi territory and Saudi airstrikes in Yemen have resumed this year. The latest exchange also threatens the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, the shipping route linking the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden.

Latest · Global Affairs · G7 — Paris Declaration12 November 2026

G7 Ministers in Paris Reaffirm Support for Ukraine, Condemn China’s Military Aid to Russia and Warn Over Taiwan’s East Coast

Foreign ministers of the Group of Seven have closed talks in Paris with a joint declaration reaffirming support for Ukraine and criticising China both for supplying Russia with weapons that enable the war to continue and for increasingly assertive activity in the waters east of Taiwan. The ministers said China’s actions off Taiwan’s east coast, where regular coast guard patrols have stepped up since June, threaten regional stability and freedom of navigation, and they stressed the importance of peace and stability across the Taiwan Strait. China, which regards Taiwan as its own territory and has never renounced the use of force, did not immediately respond to a request for comment and recognises no Taiwanese sovereignty claims. Taiwan’s defence minister Wellington Koo said on Tuesday, after a reported rise in Chinese activity, that Beijing is pursuing what he described as area-denial operations around the island, where Chinese forces already operate on a daily basis. The United States, Britain, France and Germany have all previously raised concern about the east-coast patrols.

Latest · Asia-Pacific · ASEAN — Manila Summit Preview12 November 2026

ASEAN’s Manila Summit Opens Next Week Under the Shadow of the Iran War, US–China Rivalry and the Myanmar Crisis

Southeast Asian leaders will meet in Manila from 15 to 17 November for the 49th ASEAN summit, with major powers joining against one of the bloc’s most complex backdrops in years: the Iran war battering regional economies through fuel prices, a sharpening US–China rivalry, and unresolved conflicts at home. The 11-member Association of Southeast Asian Nations — founded in 1967 and expanded last year when Timor-Leste became its 11th member — is also grappling with Myanmar’s civil war, fighting between Thailand and Cambodia, and competing claims in the South China Sea. Energy security, artificial intelligence and transboundary pollution top the expected agenda, and the bloc this year added a facility to provide rapid liquidity during sudden shocks such as natural disasters and pandemics. On the sidelines, the White House says President Donald Trump will hold a special summit with ASEAN leaders on 17 November and meet Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Jr., while South Korea’s President Lee Jae Myung begins his regional trip in Manila on 16 November.

Gold Falls to a One-Week Low as a Firm Dollar and Rate-Hike Bets Weigh, While Foreign Money Pours Back Into Indian Equities

Gold prices touched their lowest level in more than a week on Thursday as stronger-than-expected US inflation data and hawkish remarks from Federal Reserve officials lifted the dollar and cemented expectations of an interest-rate increase next month. Latest · Africa · Ebola — Kenya Countdown Begins12 November 2026

Kenya Discharges Its Last Ebola Patient and Starts the 42-Day Countdown, as DR Congo’s Case Count Passes 11,000

Kenya has released its last Ebola patient from treatment, a milestone that starts the standard 42-day countdown — twice the virus’s maximum incubation period — that must pass without a new case before the outbreak can formally be declared over. The step marks a turning point for Kenya’s response even as the regional picture darkens: the government of the Democratic Republic of the Congo has reported that cumulative Ebola cases there have now exceeded 11,000, keeping pressure on health systems and on cross-border screening across East Africa. Health officials stress that surveillance will stay at full strength through the countdown, since a single missed case could restart transmission, and neighbouring countries are maintaining border checks while aid agencies continue supporting treatment centres in the worst-hit Congolese provinces.

In APEC Host City Shenzhen, China’s Next-Generation Startups Still Bank on Wealthy Foreign Buyers Despite Trade Friction

As Shenzhen prepares to host the APEC summit on 18–19 November, with billboards welcoming President Donald Trump and other leaders, Reuters interviews with executives at seven startups in the city’s hardware incubator InnoX show many are doubling down on products aimed at wealthier customers overseas — from portable power stations to exoskeletons — even as trade tensions reset the terms of globalisation. China’s trade surplus topped $1 trillion in the first ten months of 2026 and is on track to beat last year’s record, and while duties on Chinese goods peaked at 145 percent in April 2025 before a truce, shipments to the United States rebounded by nearly 40 percent year-on-year in September after a Trump–Xi meeting eased tensions. The domestic picture is softer: Shenzhen’s retail sales grew just 2.3 percent between January and September, well below the municipal target and far outpaced by an 18.5 percent rise in city exports, with state-backed projects in the area now totalling more than 60 billion yuan, about $8.7 billion.
